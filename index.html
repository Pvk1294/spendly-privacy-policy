Spendly — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026 · Contact: pvk1294@gmail.com

Spendly is a personal finance app that helps you track expenses, income, and investments. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Account information includes your name, email address and age, an optional phone number, and profile photo if supplied by Google Sign-In. For email/password accounts, the backend stores a password hash. Authentication and device tokens keep you signed in and support background sync.

Transaction records include amount, currency, merchant, date, category, payment method, source sender, and available account or card reference details from the message. Imported records also include the original transaction SMS text. We also store categories, investments, watchlists, and SIP goals you create.

The app keeps preferences, cached account data, and SMS import state on your device. If you send feedback, we receive your message, your account email, and app version, Android version, device model and submission time. You can optionally attach a screenshot or other image. Our support email provider processes the message and attachment. If you contact support separately, we receive the information you include.

How SMS Data Is Handled

On Android, SMS access is optional. With your permission, Spendly scans SMS on your device to identify supported bank and payment transaction alerts, imports recent messages from the last 60 days, and detects new messages. Transaction matching uses sender and message-content rules; it is not limited to a fixed list of bank senders.

Matched transaction SMS and extracted financial information are sent to Spendly’s backend for processing and storage. The original message can contain financial details beyond the displayed amount and merchant. New matching SMS can also be sent for server-side parsing in the background when the app is closed.

On-device filters are designed to exclude OTPs, personal conversations, and non-transaction messages from automatic transaction uploads. SMS recognition is automated and can make mistakes. Review imported entries and report incorrect matches.

“Help improve SMS parsing” is a separate optional setting, off by default, in Profile › Data & Permissions. When enabled, supported transaction message text, sender, receipt time, and parsing results may be retained as parser-improvement samples. Promotional messages, OTPs, and non-transaction messages are filtered out. This data is used to improve transaction recognition and categorization.

Turn off SMS Auto-Capture in Profile › Data & Permissions to stop automatic processing while it is off, or revoke SMS permissions in Android settings. A later import or rescan can include messages received during that period. Turning off “Help improve SMS parsing” stops new sharing for that purpose after the change is confirmed, but does not recall uploads already in progress or delete previously uploaded information. Existing account records remain available.

Background Transaction Sync

While SMS Auto-Capture and SMS permissions are enabled, Android background processing can upload matching new transaction SMS without opening the app. Background delivery depends on Android and device battery restrictions. Revoking SMS access prevents SMS import as well as background capture; you can still enter transactions manually.

How We Use Your Information

We use account and financial data to authenticate you, display and categorize transactions, calculate spending insights, sync your records, and provide the finance features you choose. Notifications such as spending summaries require notification permission.

Optional parser-improvement sharing supports development of SMS parsing and categorization. We do not use SMS content for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Your account and transaction information is stored on Spendly’s backend infrastructure; it is not stored exclusively on your phone. Production app-to-backend requests use HTTPS. App authentication tokens use secure local storage; the app also maintains local preferences and cached data.

Separate raw-SMS and parser-training sample records become eligible for automatic deletion after 30 days. The backend checks for expired records on startup and daily while running. This schedule is separate from transaction records, which retain their attached source SMS.

Deleting a transaction removes it from app views and spending totals and erases its attached source SMS and linked raw-SMS/training samples from the application database. A structured record and duplicate-detection identifier remain to prevent re-importing it. Unlinked raw-SMS copies follow the separate expiry schedule. Deleting your account removes these retained account records as well.

Spendly does not connect to your bank account or ask for your net banking password or payment OTP. Imported message text may contain masked account/card references and balances supplied by the sender.

Service Providers and Data Sharing

Google/Firebase Authentication processes sign-in information when you use Google Sign-In, including profile identifiers. Firebase Authentication also collects IP addresses for security and authentication SDK/device metadata to operate its service. Backend hosting and database providers process account records, financial data, and uploaded SMS on our behalf. Our email delivery provider processes email addresses and message content for account emails and support messages.

We do not sell personal information or use SMS content for advertising. Data is processed by service providers as needed to operate Spendly. We may disclose information where required by law. The current app does not integrate advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.

Your Choices and Deletion Requests

You can view and edit your profile and transaction records in the app. SMS Auto-Capture and optional parser-improvement sharing can be changed separately in Profile › Data & Permissions. Android settings control SMS and notification permissions.

You can delete your Spendly account in Profile › Data Management › Delete Account. After confirmation, this removes your account and associated records from the application database and your Spendly sign-in identity. It does not delete your Google account or messages in your phone inbox. You can also email pvk1294@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject “Spendly account deletion”. We may need to verify ownership. Do not send bank passwords or OTPs. Contact us for help with deletion or information about backup retention and copies already processed for parser improvement.

Children’s Privacy

Spendly is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us to request removal.

Changes to This Policy

We will update the effective date when this policy changes. The published policy and the in-app policy should describe the same data handling.
